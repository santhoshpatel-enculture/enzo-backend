## About Enculture
Enculture is an AI-powered Culture Intelligence and Employee Engagement Platform for mid-to-large enterprises. It aligns people, purpose, and performance to improve retention, productivity, and organizational alignment.

## Core features
- **Pulse check-ins**: Short Teams-based check-ins for stress, workload, and sentiment.
- **360 feedback**: Multi-rater feedback for performance and leadership.
- **Culture surveys**: Customizable templates with analytics and theme clustering.
- **Feedback Speaks**: Secure channel for actionable employee feedback.
- **Dashboards**: Response rates, sentiment trends, AI narratives, and early risk signals.

## Privacy and anonymity
- Individual survey responses are never shown to managers or leadership.
- Data is aggregated at the team level (minimum five responses per segment).
- Enzo may reference enrollment and completion status only—not individual program answers.
